# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free environment for
everyone working on post-quantum cryptography.

## Standards

- Be precise about ideas and generous about people.
- Critique constructions, not contributors.
- Disagreement about soundness bounds is technical work, not personal
  conflict — bring arguments and proofs.

## Enforcement

Violations (harassment, personal attacks, doxxing) are reported privately
to the maintainers and may result in a ban from the project and its
spaces. This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant; the
full text is at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/>.
